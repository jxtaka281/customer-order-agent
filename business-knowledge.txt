CUSTOMER ORDER SHOP — BUSINESS INFORMATION

Products and unit prices
- Notebook costs NGN 2,000.
- Pen costs NGN 500.
- School bag costs NGN 10,000.

Ordering
- Customers can include multiple products in one order.
- Each product must have a positive whole-number quantity.
- Customers review their order before confirming it.
- A saved order has an order reference.

Checking an existing order
- Customers should provide the order reference from their confirmation.
- Current order details must be retrieved using the order lookup tool.

Information awaiting confirmation from the business owner
- Delivery availability, charges and delivery times.
- Opening hours.
- Returns and refunds.
- Current stock availability.
