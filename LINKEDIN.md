# Post

I’ve completed a working customer-order AI agent prototype for my automation portfolio.

Customers can ask about products, request several items in one message, review the official total, and confirm their order through chat.

I built it with Make, webhooks, HTTP tools, a data store, and a JavaScript webpage, with AI assistance during development.

The most valuable part was debugging: fixing product-name matching, teaching the agent when to call its preview tool, and preventing customer-supplied prices from replacing official prices.

My manual tests passed for preview and confirmation, repeated confirmation without a duplicate, changed orders requiring fresh approval, and declined confirmation.

This is a tested learning prototype, with further security and reliability work planned before real customer use.

I’m building practical skills in AI agents and business automation and would welcome feedback from others working in this space.
